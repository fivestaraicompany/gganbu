The meaning of GGanbu

A trusted buddy, not a sexual relationship.

“GGanbu” means a close, dependable friend—someone you can talk with openly and treat with care. The app is designed for healthy, non-sexual communication between respectful adults.

✓Friendly connection built on mutual interest and respect
✓Clear controls for visibility, blocking, and reporting

Designed for better conversations

Simple technology. Human connection.

GGanbu Chat keeps nearby communication direct and gives you practical tools to connect thoughtfully.

📡

Direct nearby chat

Bluetooth Low Energy helps nearby devices discover and connect directly. Core chats do not pass through a central chat server.

🌐

Optional translation

Use a configured Ollama-compatible endpoint to view original and translated messages together and make conversation easier.

📱

iPhone meets Android

iOS and Android use the same nearby protocol, so the people around you are not separated by their choice of phone.

🤝

Become GGanbu

When friendly interest is mutual, you become GGanbu—trusted buddies who have chosen to stay connected.

🖼️

Share the moment

Send photos as well as text directly over Bluetooth, and share everyday moments with the people you trust.

🛡️

Safety in your hands

Choose when you are discoverable, delete local conversations, and block or report abusive users. GGanbu Chat is for adults aged 18 and older.

How it works

You stay in control from hello.

Set up a profile, choose when to be visible, and decide who you want to talk with.

Set up your profile

Create a profile, grant the permissions needed for nearby chat, and choose when to become discoverable.

See people nearby

Discoverable adults in the same café, library, event, or shared space appear with approximate Bluetooth proximity.

Choose and say hello

Select a nearby profile and start a direct conversation. Optional translation can help both people feel understood.

Conversation without borders

Different languages can still feel close.

When a compatible translation endpoint is configured, GGanbu Chat can help translate conversations while keeping the original message visible.
